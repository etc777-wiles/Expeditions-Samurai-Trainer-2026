**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Expeditions: Samurai**. This trainer provides optional advantages for single-player campaigns, including improved survivability, resource control, easier exploration, and greater flexibility during the game's turn-based combat, stealth encounters, equipment management, and branching quests.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with different builds, reduce repetition, test combat strategies, explore branching story content, or enjoy a more relaxed single-player campaign.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The campaign combines real-time party positioning during exploration with turn-based encounters. Use the trainer's movement-related options only after the party is fully loaded into an area. Position characters before combat, split the crew when scouting, and use stealth to prepare ambushes without forcing movement values during transitions.

### Tools & Inventory

Expeditions: Samurai uses equipment, weapons, armor, consumables, and loadouts to define how each crew member performs. Open the inventory before activating item-related options, select the required character or item, make the desired changes, and disable the option before selling, equipping, or transferring gear.

### World & Environment

Travel across the campaign map, investigate locations, complete quests, and make decisions that influence companions and the wider story. World-map options are safest to use after a destination has been selected and the travel sequence has begun. Disable them before triggering a major quest event or loading a new region.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and supports movement, attacks, interruptions, stealth, gadgets, ranged weapons, melee weapons, and coordinated ambushes. Activate combat options after the encounter begins and a party member is selected. Use unlimited action points to test positioning and abilities, then disable the trainer before the encounter ends or the game transitions back to exploration.
